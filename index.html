My Design
Approach

01020304

  1. 01. Understand the business and user goals

    Research is the first thing I do, and the most important. It helps me understand both the user's world and the business goals at the same time, so I know exactly where to focus and why. Without that clarity, design is just guessing.

  2. 02. Structure Before Aesthetics

    I build clear flows and logical structures before focusing on visual design. A beautiful screen that confuses the user is a failed screen. Clarity comes first, then craft.

  3. 03. AI-Assisted Design

    I use AI to speed up research, explore ideas, and refine details — while keeping the real design decisions human-led.

  4. 04. Staying close to the team

    I like to stay involved with other UX teammates, developers, stakeholders, and clients while the product is being shaped. It keeps the process smoother and the final design stronger.
